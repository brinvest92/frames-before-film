BookNest micro-interactions

Four small moves for the BookNest app, live. Each one lists every move with its duration and its curve. The stylesheet booknest-micro.css carries the same numbers as tokens and as comments.

The five curves

--bn-ease-incubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)leaving

--bn-ease-springcubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)landing with a small overshoot
